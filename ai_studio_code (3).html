Agar aapka koi ek account hack hua hai, toh hacker aapke baki accounts ko bhi target kar sakta hai. Sabse pehle apne primary Gmail/Email account aur baki sensitive apps ke passwords badlein aur unme 2-Step Verification (2FA) on karein.

Platform Recovery Guides

Niche diye gaye platforms me se jisme problem hai, uspar click karke official recovery steps follow karein:

1

Email Check Karein: Apne registered email inbox me security@mail.instagram.com se aaya koi mail check karein. Agar hacker ne email badla hoga, toh aapko "Revert this change" ka option mil sakta hai.

2

Login Link Request Karein: Login screen par "Get help logging in" (Android) ya "Forgot password?" (iOS) par click karein aur apna username ya phone number dalein.

3

Identity Verification: Agar upar diye steps kaam nahi karte, toh "Need more help?" par click karein. Agar aapke account me aapki photos hain, toh Instagram aapko ek video selfie lene ko kahega jisse aapki identity verify ho sake.

Future Safety Tips (Account Safe Kaise Rakhein)

1. Strong Password Banayein

Apne passwords me symbols (@, #, $), numbers, aur mix upper-lowercase letters ka use karein. Har app ke liye alag password rakhein.

2. Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

Instagram, Snapchat aur WhatsApp par Two-Factor settings me jaakar enable karein. Isse bina OTP ya authenticator app code ke login nahi hoga.

3. Phishing Se Bachein

Kisi bhi aisi link par click na karein jo "Free followers" ya "Gift cards" dene ka dawa karti ho, yeh account hack karne ka trap ho sakta hai.

4. Login Sessions Monitor Karein

Regularly settings me jaakar "Active Logins" ya "Logged in devices" check karein aur unknown devices ko remove kar dein.
